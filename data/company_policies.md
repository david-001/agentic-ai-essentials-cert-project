# TaskFlow Pro - Employee Handbook

Welcome to TaskFlow Pro! This handbook outlines our company policies, benefits, and culture. We're committed to creating an inclusive, supportive environment where everyone can do their best work.

## Company Mission and Values

### Our Mission
To empower teams worldwide with intuitive, powerful tools that make project management effortless and collaboration seamless.

### Our Core Values

**Innovation First**: We constantly push boundaries and embrace new technologies to solve customer problems in creative ways.

**Customer Obsession**: Every decision we make starts with the customer. We listen, iterate, and deliver solutions that exceed expectations.

**Transparency**: We operate with openness and honesty, sharing information freely across the organization and with our customers.

**Work-Life Harmony**: We believe exceptional work doesn't require sacrificing personal wellbeing. Balance is essential to sustained excellence.

**Diversity and Inclusion**: We actively build teams with diverse perspectives, backgrounds, and experiences because different viewpoints make us stronger.

## Employment Basics

### Work Schedule
Standard business hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM in your local timezone, but we embrace flexible scheduling. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM, when we expect team members to be generally available for meetings and synchronous work.

Employees may adjust their schedule earlier (7 AM start) or later (11 AM start) with manager approval, as long as core hours are covered and team collaboration isn't impacted.

### Remote Work Policy
TaskFlow Pro is a remote-first company. All positions can be performed remotely from anywhere with reliable internet access. We maintain small office spaces in San Francisco, New York, and London for employees who prefer occasional in-person work.

**Remote Work Guidelines:**
- Employees may work from any location within their country of employment
- Reliable high-speed internet (minimum 25 Mbps) is required
- Dedicated workspace that allows for video calls is necessary
- International remote work requires approval and may involve tax/legal considerations
- We provide a $500 annual stipend for home office equipment
- Optional co-working space membership reimbursement up to $200/month

### Office Access
For employees near our office locations:
- Offices are accessible 24/7 with badge access
- Hot desks available on a first-come basis
- Conference rooms bookable through our internal system
- Complimentary snacks, beverages, and catered lunch on Wednesdays
- Bike storage and shower facilities available

## Compensation and Benefits

### Salary Structure
We conduct annual compensation reviews every January, with adjustments based on performance, market rates, and company performance. Mid-year adjustments may occur for promotions or significant role changes.

Salary bands are transparent and published internally. All employees can see the ranges for their role and level. We aim for 75th percentile compensation compared to similar companies in the technology sector.

### Equity Compensation
All full-time employees receive equity as part of their compensation package:

**Equity Grants:**
- Standard grants vest over 4 years with a 1-year cliff
- Vesting occurs monthly after the initial cliff period
- New hire grants are typically issued on the first day of employment
- Refresh grants are considered annually based on performance and retention
- Exercise window extends to 10 years post-termination (vs. industry standard 90 days)

**Stock Options (for roles hired before 2023):**
- Options to purchase company stock at fair market value
- Must be exercised within 10 years of grant or 10 years after departure

**RSUs (Restricted Stock Units - for roles hired 2023 onwards):**
- Direct grants of company stock
- No purchase required upon vesting
- Automatically converted to shares

### Health Insurance
We provide comprehensive health coverage with the company covering 90% of premiums for employees and 70% for dependents.

**Medical Insurance:**
- Three plan options: PPO, HMO, and High-Deductible with HSA
- Coverage begins on your first day of employment
- Network includes major hospitals and physicians nationwide
- $0 copay for preventive care
- Maximum out-of-pocket: $3,000 individual, $6,000 family (PPO)

**Dental Insurance:**
- Two cleanings per year covered at 100%
- Coverage for fillings, crowns, root canals, and orthodontics
- $1,500 annual maximum benefit

**Vision Insurance:**
- Annual eye exam covered at 100%
- $200 allowance for frames or contacts every 12 months
- Discounts on LASIK and corrective surgery

**Mental Health Support:**
- Unlimited therapy sessions through our EAP (Employee Assistance Program)
- Coverage for psychiatry and medication management
- 24/7 crisis support hotline
- Meditation app subscription (Calm or Headspace)

### Retirement Benefits
**401(k) Plan (US Employees):**
- Company matches 100% of contributions up to 6% of salary
- Immediate vesting of company match
- Access to low-cost index funds and target-date funds
- Financial planning resources available at no cost

**Pension Plans (International):**
- Equivalent retirement contributions for employees outside the US
- Compliance with local regulations and standards
- Varies by country; consult HR for specific details

### Paid Time Off

**Vacation Days:**
- 0-2 years: 20 days per year
- 3-5 years: 25 days per year
- 5+ years: 30 days per year
- Accrued monthly and available immediately
- Unused days roll over up to a maximum of 10 days

**Holidays:**
- 12 company-wide holidays per year
- Includes major US holidays plus 3 floating holidays
- Company closes between December 24-31 annually (paid time off)

**Sick Leave:**
- Unlimited sick days with manager notification
- No formal tracking required
- Extended illness beyond 2 weeks may require medical documentation
- Short-term disability coverage begins after 14 consecutive days

**Parental Leave:**
- 16 weeks fully paid for primary caregivers
- 8 weeks fully paid for secondary caregivers
- Available for birth, adoption, or foster placement
- Flexible return with option for part-time (80%) first month back
- Additional 4 weeks unpaid leave available if needed

**Sabbatical:**
- 4-week paid sabbatical after 5 years of continuous service
- Additional sabbaticals every 5 years thereafter
- Must be taken in a continuous block
- Used for travel, rest, learning, or personal projects

**Learning and Development:**
- $3,000 annual budget for courses, conferences, and certifications

- Monthly lunch-and-learn sessions with internal and external speakers

**Food and Beverage:**
- $60/month food delivery credit (DoorDash, Uber Eats, or Grubhub)

**Commuter Benefits (if using office):**

## Professional Development

### Performance Reviews
We conduct quarterly check-ins and annual comprehensive reviews:

**Quarterly Check-ins (30-60 minutes):**
- Progress toward goals discussion
- Feedback on recent work
- Career development conversation
- Adjustment of goals if needed

**Annual Review (90 minutes):**
- Comprehensive performance evaluation
- 360-degree feedback from peers and stakeholders
- Salary and equity review
- Career path discussion and goal setting for next year

### Career Progression
We have clear career ladders for each role family (Engineering, Product, Design, Sales, etc.):

**Individual Contributor Track:**
- Junior → Mid-Level → Senior → Staff → Principal → Distinguished
- Focus on technical depth and project impact
- No management responsibilities required

**Management Track:**
- Team Lead → Manager → Senior Manager → Director → VP → SVP
- Focus on people development and organizational impact
- Lateral movement between tracks encouraged

**Promotions:**
- Based on demonstrated performance at next level, not time in role
- Require nomination by manager and review by calibration committee
- Typically occur during annual review cycle
- Can happen off-cycle for exceptional circumstances

### Internal Mobility
We encourage internal career moves:
- All positions posted internally before external recruiting
- 18-month minimum in current role before transferring (waivable)
- Manager support required but not as a blocker
- Successful transfers include 30-day overlap/transition period

## Workplace Policies

### Code of Conduct
We expect all employees to:
- Treat colleagues with respect and professionalism
- Embrace diverse perspectives and backgrounds
- Communicate openly and honestly
- Assume positive intent in interactions
- Raise concerns promptly through appropriate channels

**Prohibited Behavior:**
- Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind
- Violence or threats of violence
- Substance abuse that impairs work performance
- Misuse of company resources or information
- Conflicts of interest without disclosure

### Anti-Harassment Policy
TaskFlow Pro is committed to providing a harassment-free workplace. We define harassment as unwelcome conduct based on protected characteristics including race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or genetic information.

**Reporting Process:**
- Report to direct manager, HR, or any executive
- Anonymous hotline available: 1-855-TASKFLOW-ETHICS
- All reports investigated promptly and confidentially
- No retaliation for good-faith reports
- Violations result in disciplinary action up to termination

### Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion
We actively work to build an inclusive culture:

**Current Initiatives:**
- Employee Resource Groups (ERGs) for underrepresented communities
- Unconscious bias training for all employees (annual requirement)
- Diverse interview panels for all roles
- Pay equity audits conducted semi-annually
- Inclusive language guidelines for communication
- Accessibility accommodations available on request

**Our Goals:**
- 50% representation from underrepresented groups by 2027
- Pay parity across all demographics (currently at 98%)
- 100% of employees complete DEI training annually

### Data Security and Confidentiality
As a SaaS company, we handle sensitive customer data:

**Requirements:**
- Complete security training within first week
- Use password manager for all accounts (1Password provided)
- Enable 2FA on all work accounts
- Never share login credentials
- Encrypt laptops with FileVault or BitLocker
- Report security incidents within 1 hour
- Sign NDA and confidentiality agreement

**Confidential Information:**
- Customer data and usage information
- Product roadmaps and unreleased features
- Financial performance and projections
- Employee personal information
- Security procedures and infrastructure details

### Social Media Guidelines
You're free to engage on social media, with some guidelines:
- Don't share confidential company information
- Disclose your employment when discussing TaskFlow Pro
- Make clear you're speaking personally, not for the company
- Be respectful and professional
- Don't engage in arguments representing the company
- Direct press inquiries to our communications team

## Offboarding

### Resignation
We ask for 2-4 weeks notice depending on role:
- Individual contributors: 2 weeks
- Senior leaders and managers: 4 weeks
- Counter-offers are rarely made; we respect your decision
- Exit interview conducted with HR
- Final paycheck includes unused vacation payout

### Termination
If employment ends involuntarily:
- At-will employment (US); notice periods vary internationally
- Severance may be provided based on circumstances
- COBRA healthcare continuation available (US)
- Equipment must be returned within 5 business days
- Access to systems terminated on last day

### Post-Employment
- Stock options remain exercisable for 10 years
- Non-compete agreements are not enforced in most states
- We're happy to serve as references for good performers
- Alumni network and events available
- Eligible for rehire after 6 months
