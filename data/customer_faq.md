# TaskFlow Pro - Customer FAQ

## Getting Started

### How do I sign up for TaskFlow Pro?

Visit taskflowpro.com and click "Start Free Trial" in the top right corner. You'll need to provide your work email address and create a password. We offer a 14-day free trial on all plans with no credit card required. After creating your account, you'll be guided through a quick onboarding process to set up your first workspace and project.

### What's included in the free trial?

Our 14-day free trial gives you full access to all features of the Professional plan, including unlimited users, 100GB storage per user, advanced reporting, time tracking, and priority support. No credit card is required to start your trial. At the end of the trial, you can choose to upgrade to a paid plan or downgrade to our free Starter plan.

### Can I import data from other project management tools?

Yes! We support imports from Asana, Trello, Monday.com, Jira, Basecamp, and many other tools. Go to Settings > Import Data and select your source platform. Our import wizard will guide you through the process. Most imports complete within 30 minutes depending on data volume. We'll migrate your projects, tasks, comments, attachments, and user assignments while preserving dates and relationships.

### Is there a mobile app?

Yes, we have native mobile apps for both iOS and Android. The iOS app requires iOS 14 or later and is available on the App Store. The Android app requires Android 10 or later and is available on Google Play. Both apps offer full task management capabilities, offline mode, time tracking, and push notifications. You can also use TaskFlow Pro in any mobile web browser.

### How do I invite team members?

From your workspace, click on your profile icon and select "Invite Team Members." You can invite people by entering their email addresses (comma-separated for multiple invites) or by importing a CSV file with email addresses. Invited members will receive an email with a link to join your workspace. You can also generate a shareable invite link from the same menu. Team members can sign up with the link without requiring individual email invitations.

## Account and Billing

### What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) and debit cards. Enterprise customers can pay via bank transfer (ACH or wire) with NET 30 payment terms. We also accept PayPal for annual subscriptions. All payments are processed securely through Stripe, and we never store your full card details on our servers.

### Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from Settings > Billing. When you upgrade, you'll be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of your billing cycle and gain immediate access to new features. When you downgrade, the change takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle, and you'll retain access to premium features until then. No penalties or fees for plan changes.

### How does per-user pricing work?

We charge based on the number of active users in your workspace. An active user is anyone who logs in during the billing period. Guest users (with limited view-only access) don't count toward your user total. You can add or remove users at any time. When you add users mid-cycle, we'll charge a prorated amount. When you remove users, we'll apply a credit to your next invoice.

### What happens if I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your subscription anytime from Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. Your account will remain active until the end of your current billing period. After that, you'll be moved to our free Starter plan with access to basic features. All your data is preserved for 90 days after cancellation. You can export your data at any time before or after cancellation. To permanently delete your data, contact support.

### Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or educational institutions?

Yes! We offer 50% off all paid plans for registered nonprofits, educational institutions, and open-source projects. Apply for a discount at taskflowpro.com/nonprofit or taskflowpro.com/education. You'll need to provide proof of status (501(c)(3) documentation, .edu email address, etc.). Approval typically takes 2-3 business days. The discount applies to annual subscriptions and renews automatically while you remain eligible.

### Can I get a refund?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all annual subscriptions. If you're not satisfied within the first 30 days, contact support for a full refund. Monthly subscriptions are not refundable, but you can cancel at any time to avoid future charges. We do not provide pro-rated refunds for partial months. Enterprise contracts have custom refund terms specified in your agreement.

## Features and Functionality

### How many projects can I create?

On all paid plans, you can create unlimited projects. The free Starter plan allows up to 3 active projects. Archived projects don't count toward this limit. Each project can contain unlimited tasks, subtasks, and attachments within your storage limits.

### What's the difference between tasks and subtasks?

Tasks are individual work items within a project. Subtasks are smaller units of work that belong to a parent task. You can nest subtasks up to 5 levels deep. Completing all subtasks doesn't automatically complete the parent task—you must manually mark the parent complete. Subtasks inherit the project from their parent but can have different assignees, due dates, and priorities.

### Can I set recurring tasks?

Yes! When creating or editing a task, click "Make Recurring" and choose your schedule. Options include daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or custom intervals. You can specify an end date or let tasks recur indefinitely. Recurring tasks are automatically generated based on your schedule. When you complete a recurring task instance, the next one is created immediately with the due date adjusted according to your schedule.

### How do task dependencies work?

Task dependencies let you specify that one task must be completed before another can start. When editing a task, go to the "Dependencies" section and select blocking tasks. Dependent tasks are automatically marked and can't be moved to "In Progress" until their blockers are complete. This ensures work happens in the correct sequence. Dependencies are visualized in Gantt chart view and affect critical path calculations.

### Can I customize workflows?

Yes! Each project can have custom workflows with as many columns as you need. Go to Project Settings > Workflow and add, remove, or rename columns. You can also set up automation rules that move tasks between columns based on triggers (e.g., move to "In Review" when all subtasks are complete). Templates let you save custom workflows for reuse across projects.

### How does time tracking work?

Click the timer icon on any task to start tracking time. The timer runs in the background while you work, and you can pause or stop it anytime. Time entries are logged against the task with start and end timestamps. You can also manually add time entries retroactively. All time data appears in your timesheets, which can be submitted for approval. Managers can view team timesheets and generate reports on time distribution across projects and tasks.

### Can I generate reports?

Yes! Navigate to Reports in the left sidebar to access our reporting suite. We offer pre-built reports for project progress, team workload, time tracking, task completion rates, and more. You can customize any report by filtering data, selecting date ranges, and choosing which metrics to display. Create custom reports using our drag-and-drop report builder. All reports can be exported to PDF, Excel, or CSV, and you can schedule automated report delivery via email.

### How do notifications work?

You'll receive notifications for events relevant to you: task assignments, @mentions, comments on your tasks, due date reminders, and project updates. Customize notification preferences in Settings > Notifications. Choose between email, in-app, mobile push, or browser push notifications for each event type. You can also set quiet hours to pause notifications during specific times. Project-level notification settings let you mute noisy projects while staying informed on important ones.

## Integrations

### What integrations are available?

TaskFlow Pro integrates with over 200 applications including Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace, GitHub, GitLab, Salesforce, Zoom, and many more. Browse all integrations at taskflowpro.com/integrations. Most integrations are available on Professional plans and above. To connect an integration, go to Settings > Integrations, find your app, and click "Connect." You'll be guided through the authentication process.

### How does the Slack integration work?

Our Slack integration lets you receive task updates, create tasks, and search TaskFlow without leaving Slack. After connecting (Settings > Integrations > Slack), you can link specific projects to Slack channels. Team members will see notifications when tasks are created, updated, or completed in those projects. Use slash commands like `/taskflow create` to quickly add tasks or `/taskflow find` to search. You can also convert Slack messages to tasks with one click.

### Can I connect my Google Calendar?

Yes! Connect Google Calendar in Settings > Integrations > Google Calendar. Once connected, your TaskFlow tasks with due dates automatically appear on your Google Calendar. Changes sync bidirectionally—update a due date in TaskFlow, and it updates in Calendar, or vice versa. You can choose which projects sync to Calendar and whether to sync all tasks or only those assigned to you.

### Does TaskFlow integrate with GitHub?

Yes! Our GitHub integration links commits, pull requests, and issues to TaskFlow tasks. After connecting your GitHub account, you can reference TaskFlow tasks in commit messages using task IDs (e.g., "Fixed bug #TASK-123"). Commits are automatically logged on the referenced task. You can also create TaskFlow tasks from GitHub issues and vice versa. The integration shows development activity directly on tasks, giving project managers visibility into engineering work.

### Can I build custom integrations?

Yes! We offer a comprehensive REST API and webhooks for custom integrations. API documentation is available at developers.taskflowpro.com. Our API allows you to create, read, update, and delete tasks, projects, users, and time entries programmatically. Webhooks notify your application in real-time when events occur in TaskFlow. We also support Zapier, allowing you to connect TaskFlow with 5,000+ apps using no-code automation. API access is available on Business plans and above.

## Security and Privacy

### Is my data secure?

Yes. We take security very seriously. All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 encryption. We use industry-standard security practices including regular security audits, penetration testing, and vulnerability scanning. Our infrastructure is hosted on AWS with data replicated across multiple availability zones. We're SOC 2 Type II certified and comply with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations.

### Where is my data stored?

Data is stored in secure data centers operated by Amazon Web Services (AWS). By default, data for North American customers is stored in US East (Virginia) and US West (Oregon) regions. European customers' data is stored in EU West (Ireland) or EU Central (Frankfurt). Enterprise customers can request specific data residency locations. All data is replicated across at least three availability zones for redundancy.

### Do you offer two-factor authentication?

Yes! Two-factor authentication (2FA) is available on all plans and required on Professional plans and above. Enable 2FA in Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication. We support authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password), SMS codes, and hardware security keys (YubiKey). Once enabled, you'll need both your password and second factor to log in. We provide backup codes in case you lose access to your second factor device.

### Can I control user permissions?

Yes. TaskFlow offers granular permission controls. Workspace admins can assign roles (Admin, Member, Guest) with different permission levels. Admins have full access including billing and user management. Members can create projects and tasks but can't manage workspace settings. Guests have read-only access to specific projects they're invited to. Business and Enterprise plans offer custom roles with fine-grained permissions for tasks, projects, reports, and integrations.

### How do you handle data privacy?

We are fully GDPR and CCPA compliant. We never sell your data to third parties. Data is only used to provide and improve our services. You own your data and can export or delete it at any time. Our detailed privacy policy is available at taskflowpro.com/privacy. We conduct annual privacy audits and maintain data processing agreements for Enterprise customers. Users can request data access, correction, or deletion by contacting privacy@taskflowpro.com.

### What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your data is retained for 90 days after cancellation, during which you can reactivate your account and regain full access. After 90 days, all data is permanently deleted from our active systems. Backups containing your data are deleted according to our backup retention policy (30 days for daily backups, 90 days for monthly backups). To immediately delete your data, contact support with a deletion request. We'll confirm deletion within 48 hours.

## Troubleshooting

### I can't log in. What should I do?

First, verify you're entering the correct email and password. Passwords are case-sensitive. Try resetting your password by clicking "Forgot Password" on the login page. If you have 2FA enabled, ensure your authenticator app is showing current codes (not expired). Clear your browser cache and cookies, or try a different browser. If you still can't access your account, email support@taskflowpro.com with your registered email address, and we'll help you regain access.

### Why aren't my notifications working?

Check your notification settings in Settings > Notifications. Ensure notifications are enabled for the event types you want to receive. For email notifications, check your spam folder and add noreply@taskflowpro.com to your contacts. For mobile push notifications, verify you've granted TaskFlow permission to send notifications in your device settings. For browser notifications, check that you've allowed notifications from taskflowpro.com in your browser settings. Still having issues? Contact support with details about which notifications aren't working.

### How do I recover a deleted task?

Tasks deleted within the last 30 days can be recovered from the Trash. Go to Settings > Trash to view deleted items. Find your task and click "Restore." The task will be returned to its original project and location. Tasks permanently deleted after 30 days cannot be recovered. To prevent accidental deletions, consider using task archiving instead of deletion for completed work. Enterprise plans can request custom trash retention periods up to 1 year.

### My file uploads are failing. Why?

Check that your file is under the size limit (500MB per file). Verify you haven't exceeded your storage quota (visible in Settings > Usage). Ensure your internet connection is stable—large files may timeout on slow connections. Some file types are blocked for security (e.g., .exe, .dmg). Try a different browser or disable browser extensions that might interfere with uploads. If the problem persists, contact support with details about the file size, type, and browser you're using.
