# TaskFlow Pro - Product Documentation

## Product Overview

TaskFlow Pro is a comprehensive project management and team collaboration platform designed for modern teams. Our platform combines task management, real-time collaboration, time tracking, and advanced reporting in one unified workspace. Whether you're managing a small startup or coordinating across multiple enterprise departments, TaskFlow Pro scales to meet your needs.

Founded in 2020, TaskFlow Pro has grown to serve over 50,000 companies worldwide, from five-person startups to Fortune 500 enterprises. Our mission is to make project management intuitive, efficient, and accessible to everyone.

## Core Features

### Project Management
TaskFlow Pro offers robust project management capabilities that help teams stay organized and on track:

- **Unlimited Projects**: Create as many projects as you need with no artificial limits
- **Custom Workflows**: Design workflows that match your team's processes with drag-and-drop boards
- **Gantt Charts**: Visualize project timelines and dependencies with interactive Gantt views
- **Milestone Tracking**: Set and monitor key project milestones with automatic progress updates
- **Task Dependencies**: Link related tasks to ensure work happens in the right sequence
- **Recurring Tasks**: Automate repetitive work with customizable recurring task schedules
- **Subtasks**: Break down complex work into manageable pieces with unlimited subtask nesting

### Team Collaboration
Real-time collaboration features keep your team connected and aligned:

- **Real-time Updates**: See changes instantly as team members update tasks and projects
- **Comments and Mentions**: Discuss work in context with @mentions that notify team members
- **File Attachments**: Attach files up to 500MB per file, with version history tracking
- **Team Chat**: Built-in messaging with channels for projects, teams, and direct messages
- **Video Conferencing**: Launch video calls directly from tasks with up to 100 participants
- **Screen Sharing**: Share your screen during video calls with annotation tools
- **Activity Feeds**: Stay informed with personalized activity feeds showing relevant updates

### Time Tracking
Comprehensive time tracking helps teams understand capacity and improve estimates:

- **Integrated Timer**: Start and stop timers directly from tasks with one click
- **Manual Entry**: Add time entries retroactively with detailed descriptions
- **Time Estimates**: Set estimated hours for tasks and track actual vs. estimated time
- **Billable Hours**: Mark time as billable and generate client-ready reports
- **Team Capacity**: View team member availability and workload across projects
- **Timesheet Approvals**: Managers can review and approve submitted timesheets
- **Time Reports**: Generate detailed reports showing time distribution across projects and tasks

### Reporting and Analytics
Make data-driven decisions with powerful analytics tools:

- **Project Dashboards**: Real-time dashboards showing project health, progress, and blockers
- **Custom Reports**: Build custom reports with our drag-and-drop report builder
- **Resource Utilization**: Track how team members' time is distributed across projects
- **Burndown Charts**: Monitor sprint progress with automatically updated burndown charts
- **Velocity Tracking**: Measure team velocity over time to improve planning accuracy
- **Budget Tracking**: Monitor project budgets and receive alerts when approaching limits
- **Export Options**: Export all reports to PDF, Excel, or CSV formats

### Automation
Reduce manual work with intelligent automation:

- **Rule-based Automation**: Create custom rules that trigger actions automatically
- **Task Auto-assignment**: Automatically assign tasks based on workload, skills, or rotation
- **Status Updates**: Auto-update task status based on subtask completion or due dates
- **Notifications**: Configure smart notifications that alert the right people at the right time
- **Recurring Workflows**: Automate entire workflows that repeat on schedules
- **Integration Triggers**: Trigger actions in TaskFlow based on events in connected apps

## Getting Started Guide

### Step 1: Sign Up (5 minutes)
1. Visit taskflowpro.com and click "Start Free Trial"
2. Enter your work email and create a password
3. Verify your email address
4. Complete the brief onboarding questionnaire
5. Choose your plan (14-day free trial on all plans)

### Step 2: Set Up Your Workspace (10 minutes)
1. Name your workspace (your company or team name)
2. Invite team members via email (or import from CSV)
3. Create your first project
4. Customize your project workflow (or use a template)
5. Configure notification preferences

### Step 3: Create Your First Project (15 minutes)
1. Click "New Project" from the sidebar
2. Choose a project template or start from scratch
3. Add project description and set timeline
4. Create task lists or workflow columns
5. Add your first tasks with assignees and due dates
6. Upload any relevant documents
7. Invite project stakeholders

### Step 4: Connect Your Tools (10 minutes)
1. Go to Settings > Integrations
2. Connect your most-used tools (Slack, Google Drive, etc.)
3. Configure integration settings and permissions
4. Test connections with sample actions

### Step 5: Set Up Automation (Optional, 15 minutes)
1. Navigate to Automation Rules
2. Browse automation templates
3. Activate relevant automations for your workflow
4. Create custom rules for team-specific needs
